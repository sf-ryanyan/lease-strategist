# 模板：给业主的消息（英文版；中文版按同样结构和事实生成，不要逐字翻译）

> 只读需要的那一段。语言按 M00 或总控台联系人表里业主的语言。

## 第 1 段 · 发送评估报告

```
Hi {Owner},

Thanks for the chance to look at {Address}. Attached is your free rental evaluation.
Quick summary:
• Recommended rent: USD {Market} (quick-lease USD {…} / stretch USD {…})
• Expected time to lease: about {X} days
• Top 3 actions: {1}, {2}, {3}
I'd be happy to walk you through it — would {Option 1} or {Option 2} work for a short call?
```

## 第 2 段 · 发送托管合同 + 邀请提问

```
Hi {Owner}, attached is the {property management / lease listing} agreement for {Address}, plus a one-page summary of the key terms in plain language.
Please take your time to review. Common questions owners ask are about fees, how to end the agreement, repair approvals and where rent goes — I've covered these in the summary. Happy to answer anything else before you sign.
```

## 第 3 段 · 请业主审批（修缮、维修报价、时间线）

```
Hi {Owner}, for {Address} we need your approval on the following:

| Item | Priority | Quote | Vendor | Expected benefit |
| {…} | Must-do / Recommended / Optional | USD {…} | {…} | {e.g., +USD 150/mo rent} |

Total: USD {…}. Proposed timeline: repairs {dates} → cleaning {date} → photos {date} → listing {date}.
Please reply "Approved" (or let me know which items) by {Date}. Each vacant day costs about USD {Rent/30}.
```

## 第 4 段 · 确认是否收到租金（模式 A）

```
Hi {Owner}, rent of USD {Rent} for {Month} at {Address} was due on {Date}. Could you confirm whether you've received it? Thanks!
```

## 第 5 段 · 租金到账确认（模式 B）

```
Hi {Owner}, rent of USD {Rent} for {Month} at {Address} was received on {Date} into the trust account. Your payout of USD {Net} will be sent on {Date}; the monthly statement will follow.
```

## 第 6 段 · Invoice 催款

```
Hi {Owner}, a friendly reminder that invoice {No.} for USD {Amount} ({description}) was due on {Date}. Please let me know if you have any questions.
```

## 第 7 段 · 续约意向询问（到期前 90 天）

```
Hi {Owner}, the lease at {Address} ends on {Date}. Before we contact the tenant, what would you like to do?
1) Renew at the same rent  2) Renew with an increase  3) Month-to-month  4) Not renew / other plans
I'll send a short renewal pricing sheet: current market rent, the legal maximum increase, and my recommendation.
Tenant summary: on-time payments {X}/12, {no} violations, {X} repair requests.
```

## 第 8 段 · 欠租或驱逐情况说明

```
Hi {Owner}, update on {Address}: rent for {Months} (USD {Total}) remains unpaid as of {Date}.
Steps so far: {reminders, late notice, calls}.
Options: (1) payment plan in writing, (2) negotiated move-out, (3) formal notice and legal process through an eviction attorney.
Legal steps must follow California rules exactly, so I recommend we consult {attorney} before serving any notice. Please let me know how you'd like to proceed.
```

## 第 9 段 · 申请人报告

```
Hi {Owner}, we have {X} complete applications for {Address}. Summary (in order received):
{Table from M07}
Recommendation: {Applicant A}, who {meets all written criteria: income {X}×, verified rental history, …}.
Please reply in writing with your decision so we can prepare the lease.
```

## 第 10 段 · 事故通知

```
Hi {Owner}, urgent update for {Address}: on {Date/Time}, {what happened}.
Immediate actions taken: {…}. Current status: {safe / habitable or not}.
Next steps: please contact your homeowner's insurance ({policy info if known}); we'll send photos and an incident report. Estimated repair: {…}.
```

## 第 11 段 · 调价建议

```
Hi {Owner}, {Address} has been listed for {X} days.
• Inquiries: {X} · Showings: {X} · Applications: {X}
• Feedback: {top 2–3 points}
• Comparable homes: {2–3 lines}
Recommendation: adjust rent to USD {…} {or other option}. Each vacant week costs about USD {Rent/4.3}.
```
