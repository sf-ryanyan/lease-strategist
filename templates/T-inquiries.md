# 模板：询盘 + 带看（给看房人和申请人，英文）

> 只读需要的那一段。{花括号} 里的内容从总控台或上市信息里填。发出前过一遍公平住房检查。

## 第 1 段 · 首次回复（Zillow 消息 / 邮件 / 短信）

**完整版（邮件、Zillow）**
```
Hi {Name},

Thanks for your interest in {Address / Street, City}.

• {Beds} bed / {Baths} bath, about {Sqft} sq ft {type}
• Rent: USD {Rent}/month · Deposit: USD {Deposit}
• Available: {Date} · Lease term: {Term}
• Pets: {Policy} (assistance animals are not pets)
• Utilities: {Who pays what}

Showings: I have openings on {Option 1}, {Option 2} or {Option 3}.
Which works best for you? {If can't come: "Here is a video / 3D tour: {Link}."}

Application: {How to apply} · Fee: USD {Fee} per adult applicant.
Our written screening criteria: {Link or short summary — e.g., income of {X}× rent or equivalent proof; verifiable rental history}.

Best,
{Manager name} · {Brand} · DRE #{…} · {Broker}
```

**短信版**
```
Hi {Name}, thanks for asking about {Street}! {Beds}bd/{Baths}ba, USD {Rent}/mo, available {Date}. Can you see it {Option 1} or {Option 2}? Video tour: {Link} – {Manager}
```

## 第 2 段 · 预筛问题（每个人问的完全一样）

```
To help schedule, could you share:
1. Your ideal move-in date?
2. How many people will live in the home?
3. Any pets? (Assistance animals are not pets.)
4. Does the household income meet about {X}× the monthly rent, or can you show equivalent proof of ability to pay?
5. How long would you like to lease?
```

## 第 3 段 · 常见问答（短答，按房源事实填）

| 问题 | 回答模板 |
|---|---|
| Is it still available? | Yes, it's available as of today. {Selection policy from M00 — e.g., "Complete applications are reviewed in the order received; the first one that meets our written criteria will be offered the home." OR "We review all complete applications received by {date}; unprocessed application fees are refunded."} |
| Can I move in earlier / later? | The home is available from {Date}. We may be able to consider {range}; please mention your date in the application. |
| Is the rent negotiable? | The listed rent is USD {Rent}. {If owner allows: "You're welcome to note any requests in your application."} |
| Pets? | {Policy}. Assistance animals are not pets and are handled through a reasonable accommodation request. |
| Parking? | {Spaces / garage / street}. |
| Utilities? | Tenant pays {…}; owner pays {…}. |
| Laundry / A/C? | {Facts}. |
| Furnished? | {Facts}. |
| How do I apply? | {Steps}. Each adult (18+) applies separately. Fee: USD {Fee}. Criteria: {Link}. |
| Can I see it without an agent? | {Self-showing / lockbox process, ID check} or {booked showings only}. |
| Who is the landlord? | The home is managed by {Brand}. |

## 第 4 段 · 敏感问题的公平回复

| 对方问 | 回复 |
|---|---|
| I have a service / emotional support animal. | Thank you for letting us know. Assistance animals are not pets, so no pet fee or pet deposit applies. You can send a reasonable accommodation request at any time; it is handled separately from screening and will not affect your application. |
| Do you accept Section 8 / housing vouchers? | Yes, voucher holders are welcome. We use the same written criteria for every applicant, and income requirements apply only to the portion of rent you pay. We can also accept other proof of ability to pay instead of credit history. |
| Is it good for kids / a family / older people? | I'm happy to describe the home itself: {facts — size, yard, stairs, distance to park / transit}. Everyone is welcome to apply under the same criteria. |
| What schools? | Please check the school district's address lookup ({district lookup link}) for the assigned schools, as boundaries can change. {Only list school names if the user provided them.} |
| Is the neighborhood safe? | I'd suggest checking official sources such as the local police department's crime map, and visiting at different times of day. I'm happy to share facts about the home and nearby transit and stores. |
| What kind of tenants live nearby? / Who are the neighbors? | I'm not able to comment on neighbors, but I'm happy to answer questions about the home. |

## 第 5 段 · 看房确认和提醒

**确认**
```
Great, you're booked for {Day, Date} at {Time} at {Address}.
Please bring a photo ID. {Parking / entry instructions.}
If plans change, just reply here. See you then!
```
**提前 24 小时**
```
Reminder: your showing at {Address} is tomorrow, {Date} at {Time}. Please reply "Yes" to confirm.
```
**提前 2 小时**
```
See you at {Time} today at {Address}. {Manager} – {Phone}
```

## 第 6 段 · 看房后跟进

**24 小时内**
```
Hi {Name}, thanks for visiting {Address} {yesterday/today}.
{Answer the concern they raised, e.g., "The landlord confirmed the garage fits two cars."}
If you'd like to apply: {Link}. We review complete applications in the order received; the deadline for this round is {Date/Time}.
Written criteria: {Link}. Let me know if you have any questions!
```
**没有回复，跟进一次**
```
Hi {Name}, just checking in on {Street}. Are you still interested? Happy to answer any questions.
```
**不申请时问原因**
```
Thanks for letting me know. If you don't mind sharing, what was the main reason (price, layout, location, timing)? It helps us a lot.
```

## 第 7 段 · 未选中通知

```
Hi {Name},

Thank you for applying for {Address}. The home has been leased to another applicant whose complete application was received earlier and met the written criteria.
{If fee not used: "Your application fee of USD {Fee} will be refunded within 7 days."}
We wish you the best in your search.

{Manager} · {Brand}
```
因为消费报告（信用报告等）拒绝或附加条件 → 另外发不利行动通知（adverse action notice），写明报告机构的名称、地址、电话，以及申请人有权免费索取报告和提出异议（按用户筛选平台的标准格式）。
