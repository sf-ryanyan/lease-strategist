# 模板：给租客的消息和通知（英文）

> 只读需要的那一段。正式法律通知（进屋、涨租、终止、3 天通知等）用 C.A.R. 或正式表格；这里是配套的说明消息。法规相关的内容先按 X3 核实。

## 第 1 段 · 入住款付款通知

```
Hi {Tenant names},

Congratulations! Here is your move-in summary for {Address}:

• Security deposit: USD {…}
• First month's rent ({dates}{, prorated}): USD {…}
• {Other}: USD {…}
• Less holding deposit received: − USD {…}
• Total due: USD {Total} by {Date}

Payment method: {Cashier's check payable to … / bank transfer / Zelle}.
{Payment details: provided separately by phone or secure message.}

⚠️ For your safety: we will never change payment instructions by email. If you receive any message asking you to send money somewhere else, call {Manager} at {known phone number} before paying.

Keys will be handed over once funds have cleared.
```

## 第 2 段 · 入住前提醒

```
Before your move-in on {Date}, please:
1. Set up utilities in your name, starting {Date}:
   • Electricity/Gas: {PG&E / city utility} – {how to start service}
   • Water: {Provider} {or "paid by owner"}
   • Garbage: {Provider} {or "included"}
   • Internet: your choice of provider
2. Send proof of renter's insurance (minimum liability USD {…}) by {Date}.
3. Move-in walkthrough: {Date, Time}. We will complete the move-in inspection together and take photos.
4. Smart devices: {Lock code / app access / garage remote pairing / thermostat} – {instructions}.
```

## 第 3 段 · 欢迎包

```
Welcome home to {Address}!

• Rent: USD {Rent}, due on the {Due day}. Grace period until the {Day}; a late fee of USD {Fee} applies after that (per your lease).
• How to pay: {Method}
• Maintenance requests: {Link / email / phone}. For emergencies (gas smell, fire, flooding, no heat in winter, sewage backup), call 911 if anyone is in danger, then call {Emergency number}.
• Manager / owner contact for notices: {Name, address, phone}
• Trash & recycling day: {Day}
• HOA rules: attached {if any}
• Utilities: {list}
• Move-in condition notes: please send any additional items you notice within {X} days.

We're glad to have you!
```

## 第 4 段 · 进屋通知（配合正式表格 C.A.R. NOE）

```
Notice of Entry – {Address}

We intend to enter the home on {Date} between {Start time} and {End time} for the following purpose: {repairs: … / inspection / showing to prospective tenants or buyers / appraisal}.
{Name/company} will be present. {You are welcome to be home but not required.}
Please secure pets and let us know if this time causes a problem.

Delivered on {Date} by {method}.
```
（一般提前 24 小时书面通知；邮寄的提前 6 天。紧急情况除外。例行检查要租约有约定或租客同意。）

## 第 5 段 · 报修信息收集

```
Thanks for reporting this. To send the right technician, please reply with:
1. What's happening and where exactly?
2. When did it start? Is it constant or on and off?
3. Photos or a short video (and a photo of the model/serial label, if it's an appliance)
4. Times you're available for a visit this week
5. May the technician enter if you're not home? Any pets?
{Quick checks you can safely try: reset the breaker / press the GFCI reset button / …}
```

## 第 6 段 · 维修完成确认

```
Hi {Name}, {Vendor} reports the {issue} at {Address} was completed on {Date}. Could you confirm everything is working? If not, just reply and we'll follow up.
```

## 第 7 段 · 交租提醒（到期前 X 天，M00 设置，默认 3 天）

```
Friendly reminder: rent of USD {Rent} for {Month} is due on {Due date}. Payment method: {Method}.
The grace period ends on {Date}; after that a late fee of USD {Fee} applies per your lease. Thank you!
```

## 第 8 段 · 到期日还没收到

```
Hi {Name}, we haven't received {Month} rent yet. If you've already paid, please disregard and send us a confirmation. The grace period ends on {Date}; after that, a late fee of USD {Fee} will apply. Let us know if you have any questions.
```

## 第 9 段 · 书面迟交通知

```
Late Rent Notice – {Address}

Rent of USD {Rent} for {Month} was due on {Due date} and has not been received as of {Date}.
Per your lease, a late fee of USD {Fee} now applies. Total now owed: USD {Total}.
Please pay by {Date} using {Method}. If you are having difficulty, please contact us today so we can discuss options with the owner.
```
（这不是 3 天付租或搬离通知。正式通知见 M16。）

## 第 10 段 · 违约友好提醒

```
Hi {Name}, we've received {a report / noticed} that {issue, e.g., a vehicle is parked on the lawn} at {Address} on {Date}. Section {X} of your lease says {rule}. Could you please {fix} by {Date}? Thanks for your help!
```

## 第 11 段 · 书面警告

```
Lease Violation Notice – {Address}

On {Dates}, {describe facts}. This violates Section {X} of your lease: "{quote}".
Please correct this by {Date}. If the issue continues, further action may be taken under your lease and California law.
```
（再下一步的正式通知先找律师或 broker。）

## 第 12 段 · 一般通知（HOA、停水停电、除虫、施工）

```
Notice – {Address}

{What}: {e.g., water shut-off for pipe repair / pest control treatment / HOA painting}
When: {Date, Time}
What you need to do: {e.g., store water / clear cabinets / move cars}
Questions: {Contact}
```

## 第 13 段 · 续约邀请

```
Hi {Name},

Your lease at {Address} ends on {Date}. We'd love for you to stay!
Renewal offer: {New term} at USD {New rent}/month starting {Date}. {Notice: the increase is within applicable legal limits.}
Please reply by {Date} to let us know if you'd like to renew. {Formal notice of change in terms is attached, if applicable.}
```

## 第 14 段 · 确认搬出日期

```
Hi {Name}, we've received your notice to move out. Confirming your move-out date: {Date}.
You have the right to request a pre-move-out inspection, which lets you see possible deductions and fix them before you leave. Would you like to schedule one? It can be done up to two weeks before move-out.
Please also send your forwarding address for the deposit statement.
```

## 第 15 段 · 退房指引

```
Move-out checklist for {Address}:
• Leave the home as clean as at move-in (normal wear and tear is fine).
• Remove all belongings and trash.
• Return all keys, remotes and access cards: {list from key receipt}.
• Stop or transfer utilities in your name as of {Date}.
• Send your forwarding address.
• Final walkthrough: {Date, Time}.
```

## 第 16 段 · 押金明细（随附件寄出）

```
Security Deposit Itemized Statement – {Address}
Move-out date: {Date} · Deposit received: USD {…}

Deductions:
1. {Item} – USD {…} – {reason / lease section} – {receipt attached / good-faith estimate}
   Done by: {vendor name, address, phone} (required if the invoice doesn't show them; for work by the owner or staff: hours × hourly rate)
2. …
Total deductions: USD {…}
Refund to you: USD {…}, sent on {Date} by {method}.
Attached: invoices/receipts and before/after photos ({link / email attachment / USB}).

{If any work is not finished: "Item X is a good-faith estimate; receipts will be sent within 14 days of completion."}
Questions: {Contact}
```

## 第 17 段 · 管理者变更通知

```
Notice – {Address}

Starting {Date}, {the home will be managed by {New manager} / the owner will manage the home directly}.
Rent payments: {how / to whom}. Maintenance requests: {contact}. Notices: {name, address, phone}.
Your security deposit of USD {…} is held by {who}. Your lease terms do not change.
```

## 第 18 段 · 紧急情况：给租客的安全指引

**燃气味**
```
Please leave the home right now with everyone (and pets). You can leave the door open on your way out.
Do NOT switch lights or appliances on or off, do NOT use lighters or matches, and do NOT use your phone or start a car inside the home or garage.
Once you are outside and safe, call 911 or the gas company's 24-hour emergency line (PG&E: 1-800-743-5000).
If the gas is shut off, please don't turn it back on yourself – we will arrange a licensed technician. I'm calling you right after this.
```
**大量漏水**
```
If water is leaking a lot, please turn off the main water shut-off valve ({location}). Move valuables away from the water and avoid using electrical outlets near wet areas. A plumber is on the way – please send photos when you can.
```
**火灾 / CO 报警器响**
```
Leave the home immediately and call 911. Do not go back inside until the fire department says it is safe. Let me know when everyone is safe.
```
（PG&E 电话发出前请核实；不是 PG&E 服务区的，换成当地燃气公司的紧急电话。）
